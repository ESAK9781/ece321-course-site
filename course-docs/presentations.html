ECE 321: 5-Minute Presentations Guide & Schedule

Guidelines, scheduling dates, and detailed prompt questions for all 13 practical electronics briefings.

All Course Docs
Presentation Format: 5 minutes strict time limit. 5–7 PowerPoint slides typically recommended, including one slide documenting all references and sources used. Graded on technical completeness, oral/visual quality, and professional delivery. Counts 2% towards the final course grade.

Presentation Detailed Prompts & Required Questions

Presentation 1: What is a PCB(printed circuit board)? L15 · Sep 17,21

  • What is a PCB(printed circuit board)?
  • Why do we prefer PCB’s over breadboards?
  • Show substrate, copper layers, solder mask, and silk screen for a two-layer board
  • What is the substrate made from?
  • What is the silkscreen layer used for?
  • What colors are used for the solder mask?

Presentation 2: What are PCB traces? L16 · Sep 22, 23

  • What are PCB traces?
  • Show for 2-layer board green traces for top of board, red for bottom of board
  • Define what is meant by 0.5oz, 1oz, or 2oz (copper) for trace thickness. How does this relate to an actual trace thickness?
  • Show how to compute trace resistance
  • Define what is meant by a mil in PCB
  • Show how to estimate required trace width for a maximum anticipated current
  • https://www.digikey.com/en/resources/conversion-calculators/conversion-calculator-pcb-trace-width

Presentation 3: Define what is a PCB via. Show examples of blind and buried vias. L18 · Sep 28, 29

  • Define what is a PCB via. Show examples of blind and buried vias.
  • Define what is a through-hole component or a surface-mount component
  • Define what is a PCB component pad and what is different for a through-hole vs surface-mount component
  • What is an annular ring?
  • What is a component footprint? How do they differ from a component symbol? Where can you get a footprint for a given component?

Presentation 4: What is the difference between radial and axial components (capacitor as an example)? What are the advantages of one over the other in a PCB? L21 · Oct 6, 7

  • What is the difference between radial and axial components (capacitor as an example)? What are the advantages of one over the other in a PCB?
  • What are PCB standoffs and why do we want to use them?
  • What is a PDIP? What is the standard lead spacing for an IC package like the LM741 op amp?
  • What are thermal vias and how can they be used with an IC?

Presentation 5: What is the difference between the schematic and the layout in PCB design? L25 · Oct 19, 20

  • What is the difference between the schematic and the layout in PCB design?
  • What are air wires?
  • What is auto-routing? Why do people sometimes prefer not to use auto-routing?
  • What is a ground plane? Why do you want to use one? What signals do you usually associate with a ground plane?
  • What layout techniques are preferred to minimize parasitic inductance and capacitance? Why do we sometimes need to minimize them?

Presentation 6: Show examples of different PCB connectors: Banana jack, BNC coaxial, terminal blocks, headers, barrel jack, RJ45, JST, IDC ribbon cable, USB, D-sub, molex. Describe why you might want to use one over another. Why are connectors preferred over wires? L28 · Oct 27, 28

  • Show examples of different PCB connectors: Banana jack, BNC coaxial, terminal blocks, headers, barrel jack, RJ45, JST, IDC ribbon cable, USB, D-sub, molex. Describe why you might want to use one over another. Why are connectors preferred over wires?

Presentation 7: Describe the standard output files of a PCB design package: gerber files, drill files, and fabrication drawings L29 · Oct 29, 30

  • Describe the standard output files of a PCB design package: gerber files, drill files, and fabrication drawings
  • Identify different PCB design packages and describe the advantages and limitations of each. Identify different PCB manufacturers and determine the typical cost of a 6” by 4” 2-layer or 4-layer board. Identify companies that will create the board, stuff it, and solder it

Presentation 8: What are Design Rule Checks in a PCB layout tool? What are examples? L30 · Nov 2, 3

  • What are Design Rule Checks in a PCB layout tool? What are examples?
  • What are creepage and clearance?
  • What are PCB test points and why might you include them?

Presentation 9: Distinguish different types of capacitors: tantalum, polypropylene, ceramic, electrolytic, polyester, film, and multilayer ceramic. What are typical applications, ranges of values, and operating characteristics? L31 · Nov 4, 5

  • Distinguish different types of capacitors: tantalum, polypropylene, ceramic, electrolytic, polyester, film, and multilayer ceramic. What are typical applications, ranges of values, and operating characteristics?
  • Show how the ceramic capacitor code works
