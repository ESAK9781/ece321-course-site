Multisim Installation & Activation Guide

Official step-by-step instructions for installing and activating National Instruments Multisim Education Edition with USAFA licensing.

Step-by-Step Activation Instructions

  1. Open the NI Product Activation Portal:
    Navigate to: NI License Activation Portal ↗
    Enter your First and Last Name, USAFA as Organization, your official cadet email, and the serial number your instructor gave you.

    Note: You must enter your serial number before you can select the product you want to activate.

  2. Select Product Edition:
    After entering the serial number, select Multisim Education Edition. The portal will automatically populate your operating system and product version fields.
  3. Obtain Your Unique Computer ID:
    To find your Computer ID, you must download and run the NI License Manager:
    Download link: NI License Manager Download ↗
    Once installed, open NI License Manager, click on Computer in the top menu bar, and view your 16-character Computer ID.
  4. Generate & Receive Activation Code:
    Submit the Activation Code Generation Form. National Instruments will send your 20-character Activation Code via email.
  5. Download Multisim & Apply Code:
    Download the Multisim software installer from:
    National Instruments Multisim Software Download ↗
    Run the installer, enter your Activation Code when prompted, and complete the installation wizard by clicking Next / Accept.
