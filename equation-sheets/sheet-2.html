Threshold & Overdrive Voltage

Overdrive Voltage (Vov)
Vov=VGS−Vt

Vt is the threshold voltage. Typical values: 0.3 V ≤ Vt ≤ 4.0 V.

Channel Charge & Oxide Capacitance
|Qchan|=Cox·W·L·Vov(for VDS small)

Cox=εoxtox=3.9ε0tox (for SiO2, tox: oxide thickness).

Process Transconductance Parameter (k'n)
kn′=μn·Cox(A/V²)

Determined solely by manufacturing process and fabrication technology.

MOSFET Device Transconductance Parameter (Kn)
Kn=kn′·(WL)(A/V² or mA/V²)

Aspect ratio W/L is the designer's primary sizing knob.

2. Modes of Operation & NMOS I-V Characteristics Lessons 8–10

Mode 1: Cutoff

Cutoff Region

Condition:
VGS<Vt  (i.e. Vov≤0)

ID=0 A

No conductive inversion channel connects Drain and Source.

Mode 2: Linear Triode

Deep Triode (Resistor)

Condition:
VDS<50 mV≪Vov

ID=Kn·Vov·VDS
rDS=VDSID=1KnVov

FET acts as a voltage-controlled linear resistor.

Mode 3: Parabolic Triode

Triode Region

Condition:
50 mV<VDS<Vov

ID=Kn[VovVDS−12VDS2]

Channel charge Source-side ∝ Vov.
Channel charge Drain-side ∝ (Vov − VDS).
Average charge: Vov → Vov − ½ VDS.

Mode 4: Saturation (Active Region for Amplification)

Saturation Region (Pinched-Off Channel)

Condition: VGS≥Vt  and  VDS≥Vov  (Equivalently: VGD≤Vt)

Ideal Saturation Current
ID=12KnVov2=12Kn(VGS−Vt)2

Current is independent of VDS when channel length modulation is neglected.

With Channel-Length Modulation (λ)
ID=12KnVov2(1+λVDS)
λ=1VAro=VA+VDSID≈VAID=1λID

VA: Early Voltage (intercept of extrapolated saturation characteristics on −VDS axis).

Double-Subscript Notation Rules

The voltage between two nodes is defined as the potential of the first subscript relative to the second subscript:

VGS=VG−VS
VDS=VD−VS
VGD=VG−VD=VGS−VDS
Key Relationship: To maintain the transistor in Saturation, the Drain-to-Gate junction must not be forward biased by more than Vt:
VGD ≤ Vt  ⇔  VDS ≥ VGS − Vt = Vov

5. NMOS Transistor DC Analysis Procedure Lesson 10

Four-Resistor Bias Network Equations

ParameterFormula
Gate Voltage VGVG=VCC·RG2RG1+RG2
Source Voltage VSVS=RS·ID
Drain Voltage VDVD=VCC−RD·ID
Gate-to-Source VGSVGS=VG−VS=VG−RSID
Drain-to-Source VDSVDS=VD−VS
Gate-to-Drain VGDVGD=VG−VD
Overdrive VovVov=VGS−Vt
Standard Solution Technique:
  1. Calculate open-circuit gate voltage VG using the voltage divider (since gate current IG = 0).
  2. Assume the device is operating in Saturation: ID=12Kn(VG−RSID−Vt)2.
  3. Solve the resulting quadratic equation for ID (select the root yielding VGS > Vt).
  4. Calculate VD and VS, then compute VDS = VD − VS.
  5. Confirm Saturation: Verify that VDS≥Vov (or VGD≤Vt). If not, re-solve assuming Triode mode.
